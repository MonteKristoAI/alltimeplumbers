## Direction tips

- **Daylight is your friend.** Shoot between 9am and 4pm, bright shade preferred over direct sun. If you're indoors, open every blind in the room.
- **Hold the phone steady.** Use both hands. If using your friend, ask them to brace elbows on their chest.
- **Take 3-5 frames per shot.** We'll pick the best one.
- **Wear the company shirt** in every shot Pete is visible in.
- **Truck should be clean.** Wash it the morning of the shoot.
- **No staged action.** If you're soldering — solder real. If you're under a sink — be actually under a sink. The camera reads "fake" within 2 seconds and tanks trust signal worse than stock photography.
- **Keep customer privacy.** No customer faces, no customer addresses on signage, no license plates in frame.
